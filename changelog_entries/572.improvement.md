Pages GuPPy opens, such as Label Stores, the results views and the Visualization GUI, now open as tabs inside the GuPPy browser tab beside the input parameters, instead of in new browser tabs. Preprocess, Remove Artifacts and PSTH Computation open their results tab as soon as they finish, and **View results →** under the step opens it again once closed. Every step now shows a status line under its button: not run yet, running with its progress bar, or done or failed with the time.
