Neurophotometrics channels are now named after the source file, excitation wavelength and region column they come from — `signals_470nm_G2` in place of `file0_chod3` — and only region columns become stores, so the `Stimulation`, `Output0/1` and `Input0/1` digital lines are no longer channels of their own. A `storesList.csv` saved by an earlier run of an NPM session names the old identifiers and will need re-labeling. `.npm_params.json` now records, under `stores`, the file, excitation wavelength or interleave position, data column and timestamp column each channel was read from.
